    Play Block Breaker free online

    Block Breaker is a free brick breaker game you play right in your browser. Bounce the ball off your paddle, smash every brick and catch falling power-ups. It works on phone, tablet and desktop, with no download and no sign-up.

    Game modes

    • Classic: clear every brick to finish a level. There are 8 hand-made levels (including a heart and a rainbow gate), then endless new levels that get harder.
    • Endless: new rows of bricks keep dropping from the top. Break them fast. If the bricks reach your paddle, the game is over. See how long you can last.
    • Difficulty: pick Easy, Normal or Hard. It changes your lives, the ball speed, the paddle size and how often power-ups drop.

    Controls

    • Desktop: move the mouse (or use the arrow keys or A and D) to move the paddle. Click or press Space to launch the ball and shoot lasers. Press P or Esc to pause.
    • Phone and tablet: drag anywhere on the game to move the paddle. Tap to launch the ball and shoot lasers. Turn your device sideways or stay in portrait, both work.
    • Full screen: use the full screen button while you play (not available on iPhone).

    Bricks

    • Colored gem bricks: break in 1 hit.
    • Framed bricks with dots: need as many hits as they have dots. Cracks show the damage.
    • Steel bricks: cannot be broken. You do not need to clear them to finish a level.
    • Bomb bricks: explode and damage every brick around them. Chain them for big combos.

    Scoring and tips

    • Break bricks in a row without touching the paddle to build a combo and multiply your points.
    • Hit the ball with the edge of the paddle to send it at a sharp angle. Hit it with the middle to send it straight up.
    • Aim at bomb bricks to clear big areas at once.
    • Keep a Safety net or an extra life in reserve for the hard levels.

    Questions

    Is Block Breaker free?

    Yes. Open the page and play. There is nothing to download or install.

    Does Block Breaker work on mobile and tablet?

    Yes. Drag your finger to move the paddle and tap to launch the ball. It works in portrait and landscape.

    How do I get power-ups?

    Some broken bricks drop a colored capsule. Move your paddle under it to catch it.

    What is the difference between Classic and Endless mode?

    In Classic you clear every brick to finish a level. In Endless new rows of bricks keep dropping and the game ends when they reach your paddle.

    How do I use the laser?

    Catch the red L capsule. While it is active, click, tap or press Space to shoot lasers from the paddle.
