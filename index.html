Play Block Breaker online

Play Block Breaker online, free in your browser. This is a block breaker game free online — no download, just press Play. Move the paddle, bounce the ball, and smash every brick. Some people write the name as one word, BlockBreaker — it is the same game.

Choose Classic mode and clear all the blocks to beat each of the 8 hand-made levels, then keep going as harder levels are built for you. Or choose Endless mode and see how long you can survive as new rows of blocks drop from the top.

How to play Block Breaker

Block Breaker play is simple: keep the ball in play and break all the bricks. Most bricks break in one to three hits. Steel bricks cannot be broken, so bounce around them, and bomb bricks explode the bricks next to them.

  • Desktop: move with the mouse or the arrow keys (or A and D). Click or press Space to launch the ball and to shoot. Press P or Esc to pause.
  • Phone and tablet: drag to move the paddle and tap to launch the ball.

Modes, difficulty and power-ups

Pick Easy, Normal or Hard before you play. Easy gives you 5 lives, Normal gives you 3, and Hard gives you 2, with a faster ball and a smaller paddle as the difficulty goes up. Your best score is saved for each mode and difficulty.

Broken bricks sometimes drop a power-up: Wide paddle, Twin ball, Multi-ball, Slow ball, Fireball, Laser, Catch ball, Giant ball, Bomb ball, Double score, Safety net and Extra life. Watch out for Shrink — it makes your paddle smaller. Breaking bricks in a row builds a combo that multiplies your score.
